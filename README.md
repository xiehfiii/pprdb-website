# PPRBD

An evidence-curated catalogue of **plant-derived peptide–receptor physical binding**. The 3 October 2026 snapshot contains 112 records from 11 plant species. The website is published at [www.pprdb.com](https://www.pprdb.com/).

The downloadable [`plant_LR_final_verified.tsv`](plant_LR_final_verified.tsv) gives, for every record, the tested peptide and receptor or receptor complex, primary DOI, experiment and figure, audit depth, and an interpretation boundary. IDs `PPR-1` through `PPR-86` remain stable; newly admitted records are appended as `PPR-87` through `PPR-112`.

Inclusion requires a same-species original study reporting a physical wet-lab binding experiment. Gene expression, genetic interaction, pathway response, orthology, docking, or a screen without pair-level binding are not enough. We label binding to an isolated receptor separately from binding measured only for a receptor complex. In-vitro binding is not automatically evidence for signalling in planta.

The original 86 records were re-audited; three receptor-complex descriptions (`PPR-8` to `PPR-10`) were corrected to the isolated receptor contacts that the primary figures actually measured. None of the 86 was deleted on this pass. Twenty-six assay-supported records were added. Audit depth is not uniform: several publisher figures could not be independently rendered, and those rows say so explicitly. This is a dated literature snapshot, **not** a guarantee that every eligible article has been discovered.

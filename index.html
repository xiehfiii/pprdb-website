What qualifies?

Each admitted row names a plant-derived ligand and a receptor or receptor complex from the same species, with an original-study physical-binding assay.

Genetics, transcript abundance, ROS responses and orthology alone were not used to establish a binding pair.

Read the evidence carefully

Binding to an isolated receptor and binding to a multicomponent complex are displayed separately. An in-vitro interaction does not automatically establish signalling in planta.
